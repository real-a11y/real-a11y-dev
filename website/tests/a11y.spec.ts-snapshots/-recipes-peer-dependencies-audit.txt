This page is the matrix."

  paragraph
    text "Several of the edge cases above (especially in Storybook and SSR apps) boil down to"
    strong "having two copies of React resolved on the page"
    text ". The manager mixes React internals from one copy with jsx-runtime calls against another, and hooks die."
